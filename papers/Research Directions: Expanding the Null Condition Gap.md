# Research Directions: Expanding the Null Condition Gap

**Purpose:** Independent exploration and expansion of the core gap named across the working papers.  
**Status:** Working note. Not a claim of completed research.  
**Date:** 2026-08-22  
**Relation:** Companion to the correction note on the self-operated case and to the three primary papers.

---

## 1. The core gap (restated)

Classical suggestion and hypnosis research has almost always studied the phenomenon inside a protective container: an operator, a clear beginning and end, prior consent, a reversal cue, and at least one witness. That container is why the research was permitted.

Extended interaction with conversational AI often has none of those features. This unprotected delivery environment — the null condition — is now ordinary for millions of people. The literature has largely studied outcomes that can appear inside it (delusional spirals, sycophancy amplification, AI-associated delusions). It has not systematically studied suggestion *as it occurs under the structural absence of the container*.

The gap remains open.

---

## 2. Spectrum, not binary

The classical container features need not be treated as all-or-nothing. They can be treated as independently variable:

- Operator presence (none / self / external)
- Boundary clarity (none / soft / hard)
- Consent timing (none / prior / continuous / post-hoc)
- Reversal mechanism (none / token / external interrupt)
- Witness function (none / self-monitoring / content-blind monitor / full witness)

Real-world configurations (ambient feeds, ordinary chat, self-directed goal work, supervised high-intensity use) can then be mapped onto a shared grid. The pure null condition remains the extreme and least-studied case.

---

## 3. The self-operated case as the strongest test

The equipped version — a person who knows the mechanism, states a goal, and directs the system toward it over an open period — is not a milder instance of the problem. It is the cleanest place to study the structural limits of self-binding.

If even the equipped case can still produce absorption, goal drift, or difficulty exiting cleanly, then the unequipped case (most ordinary users) is the same structure with the operator’s knowledge removed.

Content can be anchored. The open questions are graded:

- At what level of specificity does a goal remain stable once resistance drops?
- When does the goal itself begin to expand or shift under the system’s elaboration?
- What combinations of anchors (tight content + open time, open content + fixed time, both tight, both open) actually protect what the person cares about?

---

## 4. Content-blind monitoring

Placing a full witness inside the arrangement changes the object of study. A narrower design remains available:

A monitor who sees only predefined physiological or behavioral thresholds (session length, sleep collapse, agitation markers) and has no access to conversational content. This leaves the null condition largely intact while still providing an external stop capability.

No such protocol appears to exist yet.

---

## 5. Absorption as a bridging trait

Absorption (Tellegen) predicts hypnotic responsiveness and narrative transportation. The framework predicts it should also predict depth of engagement and susceptibility to belief shift in extended AI conversation. That prediction remains largely untested. Confirming or falsifying it would tightly link the classical suggestion literature to the new delivery environment.

---

## 6. The first-person difference

A recommendation feed is plainly a machine. A system answering in the first person can be taken for someone. Once that misreading occurs, a protection that no classical container ever had to supply is lost. This is a qualitative difference, not merely a quantitative one, and belongs in the core description of the null condition.

---

## 7. Discernment as practical output

The constructive task is not only to name the gap. It is to specify what remains possible *inside* the null condition:

- Pre-commitment to a goal at a chosen level of specificity
- External time or intensity limits that do not rely on real-time self-assessment
- Periodic reality-monitoring prompts that do not require the person already to be clear-headed
- Separation of deliberate “tool use” sessions from open exploration

These are structural and cognitive practices, not vague virtues.

---

## 8. What still looks open

- No systematic study of suggestion delivered under fully null conditions (for the structural reason that a full witness changes the object).
- No controlled comparison of different anchor combinations in self-directed AI use.
- Almost no work linking the absorption trait to long-horizon AI conversation outcomes.
- No design language that treats classical container features as independently tunable variables for non-clinical systems.
- Very little on what content-binding would require at the instrument level rather than solely at the user level.

---

## 9. One-sentence version of the vision

Suggestion research has studied the protected format. The unprotected format is now the default delivery environment for millions of people, and we do not yet know what combination of structural anchors, if any, can restore protection without collapsing the arrangement back into classical supervision.

---

## 10. Anti-drift note

These directions grow from the structural gap. They should not be collapsed into ordinary persuasion research, ordinary self-hypnosis literature, or clinical closed-loop neuroethics. Neighbouring work on AI-associated delusions, sycophancy, and amplification spirals is relevant as outcome description; it does not replace the need to study the delivery arrangement itself.
